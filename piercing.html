Essential Guidance

Preparation & Aftercare

Before Your Appointment

  • Ensure you have had a light meal and are well hydrated before arriving.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise or direct sun exposure immediately prior to your visit.
  • Bring any specific inspiration images of ear curations you admire.

Aftercare Guidance

  • Clean front and back of the piercing twice daily with sterile saline solution.
  • Do not twist, rotate, or pull on the starter jewellery while healing.
  • Avoid swimming in chlorinated pools or natural bodies of water for the first two weeks.
  • Keep hair products, lotions, and perfumes away from the fresh piercing site.
  • Our piercing specialists provide aftercare guidance at Madukani and Mori anytime.
